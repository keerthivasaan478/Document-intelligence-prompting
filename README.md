This repository houses prompt engineering files designed to automate the extraction and verification of data from business documents.  

Included Files & Their Purpose

SMBinsurancedocprompting.md

Purpose: Contains prompts specifically formatted for parsing Small and Medium-sized Business (SMB) insurance documents.

Use Case: Guides Large Language Models (LLMs) or document intelligence APIs to accurately extract policy numbers, coverage limits, premium amounts, and insured party details from standard commercial insurance paperwork.


signaturedetectionprompt.md

Purpose: Contains prompts engineered to locate and verify signatures within a document.

Use Case: Used in compliance and intake workflows to automatically flag whether a contract, application, or agreement has been properly signed by the required parties before further processing.
